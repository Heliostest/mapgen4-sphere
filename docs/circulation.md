# Evolving wind and surface-ocean circulation

Normal Live planet startup enables evolving circulation. In the paused editor, enable **Evolving wind & ocean circulation** in **Ice, ocean & vegetation**. The switch generates climate immediately and restarts its age: temperature, frozen cover, vegetation and seasonal template winds are ready without Play. Atmospheric perturbations and ocean-loop memory start at zero; Play evolves these increments. The Wind layer displays the actual vectors used by moisture transport. Environment comparison → Ocean currents displays the captured ocean vectors; its baseline can belong to another mode, identified in Captured parameters. Original map remains available.

The optional model retains the seasonal wind template and integrates an east/north perturbation on the equal-area climate grid. A diagnostic potential `Phi = -25 × T / radiationScale` supplies a horizontal temperature-gradient force. This illustrative closure inverts the authored exponential altitude factor, including its cap; it is not a measured or prognostic pressure field. Spherical east/north distances wrap longitude and use one-sided gradients at the polar rows. Coriolis uses signed planetary rotation, and drag relaxes over two days above ocean, faster over land. Constant forcing, rotation and drag are integrated analytically per fixed step. Perturbation magnitude is capped at half the configured wind magnitude; each final component respects the existing moisture transport stability bound.

Ocean circulation is a sum of closed four-cell loops, with shared faces combined before transporting heat. In evolving mode each loop relaxes over five days toward a bounded response to the actual wind circulation around it. Its current memory is saved, including when winds cease. Land, sea ice and an empty global liquid reservoir block the applied current while retaining memory. Disabling evolving mode uses the old longitude-shaped classic gyres. Heat fluxes are paired, with a shared donor bound, so closed transport preserves total sensible energy and uniform temperatures. Paused/restored diagnostics include that same bound for the next stable physical step without advancing memory.

Wind glyphs convert physical east/north vectors into the equal-area surface metric; current arrows use the equirectangular comparison metric. Their direction therefore follows the local surface tangent, including high latitudes. Globe wind components are encoded at about 0.79 m/s per byte; weak vectors can be quantized away. Comparison arrays retain full numerical precision, with arrow lengths relative to each map's maximum as stated in its legend.

This is a coarse surface circulation experiment. It does not solve prognostic atmospheric pressure or air mass, advection of momentum, three-dimensional flow, salinity, deep-ocean overturning, or a mechanical energy budget. Existing thermal diffusion still represents unresolved heat transport. Wind and ocean momentum are parameterized; conservation claims refer to the water inventory and sensible-plus-latent enthalpy ledger.

Complete simulation files preserve both perturbation vectors and every ocean-loop memory. Acknowledged-frame rollback restores them with temperatures, moisture, ice and vegetation. Dimensions, finite bounds and consistency between saved actual winds and perturbations are validated before replacement. Missing enabled memory is rejected. Older files default to classic mode; thermal-only files retain the switch but contain no active circulation.

Physical motivation: [NOAA on pressure-gradient force, Coriolis and friction](https://www.noaa.gov/jetstream/synoptic/origin-of-wind), and [NOAA on winds and surface currents](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/education/tutorial_currents/04currents1.html). These sources motivate the processes; the closure coefficients and response times here are illustrative choices, not a calibrated climate model.

Validation: kernel tests cover uniform fields, rotation sign, drag decay, finite wind bounds, fast retrograde rotation on a small planet, dry/frozen/empty-liquid barriers, heat conservation, exact resumed evolution and acknowledged rollback. Browser acceptance evolves the actual generated world through steps 3,832 and 7,665 (49 and 98 physical days), downloads/uploads state, compares the complete continued document and exact globe PNG bytes, rejects corrupt memory, checks comparison/wind/Original views and mobile/thermal-only behavior. The GPU wind fixture independently checks physical shaft and arrowhead directions, including polar rows, longitude wrap and two grid sizes.
